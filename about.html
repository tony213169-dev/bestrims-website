About Bestrims

Hong Kong roots, factory-direct production.

Two decades of custom trims — from our Tsuen Wan office to our own plant in Dongguan.

Our team ensures your logo is showcased with precision and quality. With our own automatic production lines and strict in-house QC, we provide one-stop manufacturing of high-quality 3D fashion accessories and premiums at competitive prices.

Made for brands that care about the details.

Bestrims Limited was founded in 2004 in Hong Kong. From our office in Tsuen Wan we serve apparel brands, decorators and trading companies worldwide, backed by full in-house production at our own garment-trim factory in Dongguan.

We specialise in PVC, silicone, injection, high-frequency and woven techniques — turning artwork into patches, labels, pullers and trims with dependable quality, clear communication and realistic timelines.

Whether you need a first sample or repeat production in volume, our team manages every step: artwork checking, sampling, colour matching, bulk production, QC and shipping.

Sustainability

Materials you can trust.

Bestrims is using environmental friendly materials such as Silicone, TPU, natural fabrics for production. Since 2011, we even got OEKO-TEX Standard 100, product class 1 (for baby articles) for our PVC & Silicone items. Keep on searching new materials and better techniques.

Our mission is to build up a trust-worthy, reliable industry for all the clients supporting Bestrims.
