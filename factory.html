Dongguan Factory

In-house production.

Bestrims runs its own garment-trim factory in Dongguan, China — so your trims are produced, checked and packed under one roof, with no middlemen and no guesswork.

Our factory covers moulding, injection, HF welding, weaving and transfer production, supported by automatic lines and skilled in-house teams.

Quality is checked at every stage — from material and colour matching to final packing — and our PVC & silicone items are OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified, product class 1 (baby articles).
